# Business Entities

## Purpose

This document defines the primary business entities required by the Financial Crime Analytics & Fraud Detection Platform.

The entities are derived from the data requirements and conceptual source-system design established during Step 2.

---

## 1. Core Entities

| Entity | Description | Primary Identifier | Primary Analytical Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer | Synthetic retail banking customer | customer_id | Customer behaviour and segmentation |
| Account | Banking account associated with a customer | account_id | Account activity and exposure |
| Transaction | Financial transaction performed through an account | transaction_id | Transaction and fraud analysis |
| Merchant | Merchant associated with a transaction | merchant_id | Merchant and category risk |
| Device | Device associated with transaction activity | device_id | Device behaviour and unseen-device analysis |
| Geography | Geographic context associated with activity | geography_id | Geographic behaviour analysis |
| Fraud Rule | Fraud-monitoring control | rule_id | Control effectiveness |
| Fraud Alert | Alert generated by a fraud rule | alert_id | Alert and detection analysis |
| Investigation Case | Investigation associated with suspicious activity | case_id | Investigation prioritisation |
| Fraud Outcome | Confirmed fraud classification and loss information | fraud_outcome_id | Fraud classification and loss analysis |

---

## 2. Customer

Represents a synthetic retail banking customer.

### Key identifier

- customer_id

### Business characteristics

A customer may have one or more banking accounts.

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Customer segmentation
- Customer behavioural analysis
- Customer fraud-rate analysis
- Customer baseline activity
- Identification of unusual customer behaviour

---

## 3. Account

Represents a synthetic banking account.

### Key identifier

- account_id

### Business relationship

Each account belongs to a customer.

An account may contain many transactions.

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Account-level activity analysis
- Account exposure analysis
- Customer-to-account analysis
- Account behavioural analysis

---

## 4. Transaction

Represents a single financial transaction.

### Key identifier

- transaction_id

### Business relationship

Each transaction belongs to an account and customer.

A transaction may reference:

- Merchant
- Device
- Geography

A transaction may subsequently have:

- A fraud outcome
- One or more fraud alerts

### Analytical purpose

Transaction is the **primary analytical event** in the project.

It supports:

- Fraud-rate analysis
- Transaction-value analysis
- Channel analysis
- Merchant analysis
- Device analysis
- Geographic analysis
- Velocity analysis
- Time-of-day analysis
- Behavioural analysis

---

## 5. Merchant

Represents a merchant associated with transaction activity.

### Key identifier

- merchant_id

### Business relationship

A merchant may be associated with many transactions.

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Merchant fraud analysis
- Merchant-category analysis
- Merchant risk analysis

Merchant information will be synthetic.

---

## 6. Device

Represents a device associated with transaction activity.

### Key identifier

- device_id

### Business relationship

A device may be associated with many transactions.

A device may also be associated with multiple customers where appropriate for the synthetic scenario.

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Device behaviour analysis
- Previously unseen device detection
- Customer-device relationship analysis
- Device-related risk indicators

---

## 7. Geography

Represents geographic context associated with transaction activity.

### Key identifier

- geography_id

### Business characteristics

Geographic information may include:

- Country
- Region
- City or geographic area
- Latitude band
- Longitude band

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Geographic segmentation
- Customer-region comparison
- Unusual geographic activity
- Geographic fraud analysis

Exact customer addresses will not be generated.

---

## 8. Fraud Rule

Represents a fraud-monitoring control used to identify potentially suspicious activity.

### Key identifier

- rule_id

### Business characteristics

A fraud rule may have:

- Rule name
- Rule category
- Rule description
- Rule status
- Alert threshold

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Rule-level detection analysis
- Rule-level false-positive analysis
- Control effectiveness
- Control-gap identification

---

## 9. Fraud Alert

Represents an alert generated when a fraud rule identifies potentially suspicious activity.

### Key identifier

- alert_id

### Business relationships

Each alert references:

- A transaction
- A fraud rule

A transaction may generate multiple alerts when multiple rules are triggered.

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Alert-volume analysis
- Detection analysis
- False-positive analysis
- Alert conversion analysis
- Rule-level performance analysis

---

## 10. Investigation Case

Represents an investigation associated with an alert or suspicious transaction.

### Key identifier

- case_id

### Business relationship

A case may be created from a fraud alert.

A case contains investigation information such as:

- Case priority
- Case status
- Case outcome
- Creation timestamp
- Closure timestamp

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Investigation prioritisation
- Open-case analysis
- Investigation turnaround analysis
- Investigator workload analysis

---

## 11. Fraud Outcome

Represents the confirmed fraud classification associated with a transaction.

### Key identifier

- fraud_outcome_id

### Business relationship

A fraud outcome references the relevant transaction.

A transaction may have zero or one confirmed fraud outcome record in the analytical model.

### Business characteristics

A fraud outcome may contain:

- Fraud flag
- Fraud type
- Confirmation date
- Fraud loss amount
- Fraud outcome source

### Analytical purpose

Supports:

- Confirmed fraud identification
- Fraud-loss measurement
- Fraud trend analysis
- Missed-fraud analysis
- Control effectiveness analysis
