# Data Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the data required to support the Financial Crime Analytics & Fraud Detection Platform.

The data requirements are derived from the business requirements, analytical questions, functional requirements and KPI catalogue defined during Project Initiation.

The project will use synthetic data representing a realistic but fictional UK retail banking environment.

---

## 1. Data Requirement Principles

The analytical data environment shall:

- Support transaction-level fraud analysis.
- Support customer-level behavioural analysis.
- Support account-level analysis.
- Support merchant and transaction-channel analysis.
- Support geographic analysis.
- Support device-level analysis.
- Support transaction velocity analysis.
- Support fraud-control effectiveness analysis.
- Support investigation prioritisation.
- Support executive fraud reporting.
- Support reproducible analytical workflows.
- Support documented data-quality validation.
- Contain no real customer personally identifiable information.
- Maintain clear relationships between business entities.
- Support traceability from business requirements through analytical outputs.

---

## 2. Required Business Data Domains

The project requires the following primary data domains:

| Domain | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Customer | Analyse customer characteristics and behaviour |
| Account | Analyse account-level activity and exposure |
| Transaction | Analyse transaction activity and transaction behaviour |
| Merchant | Analyse merchant and merchant-category risk |
| Device | Analyse device behaviour and previously unseen devices |
| Geography | Analyse geographic transaction patterns |
| Fraud Outcome | Identify confirmed fraudulent activity and financial losses |
| Fraud Rule | Represent fraud-monitoring controls |
| Fraud Alert | Evaluate alerts generated by fraud controls |
| Investigation Case | Support investigation prioritisation and case analysis |

The detailed relationships between these domains will be defined in the Data Model and Relationships documents.

---

## 3. Customer Data Requirements

Customer data shall support:

- Customer identification using synthetic identifiers.
- Customer segmentation.
- Customer-level transaction behaviour.
- Customer baseline activity.
- Customer fraud-rate analysis.
- Identification of unusual customer activity.

Required information includes:

- customer_id
- customer_segment
- customer_age_band
- customer_region
- customer_status
- customer_onboarding_date

No real customer names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses or other directly identifying information shall be generated.

---

## 4. Account Data Requirements

Account data shall support:

- Account-level transaction analysis.
- Customer-to-account relationships.
- Account activity analysis.
- Account risk analysis.

Required information includes:

- account_id
- customer_id
- account_type
- account_status
- account_open_date
- account_region

The data model shall support the relationship between customers and their accounts.

---

## 5. Transaction Data Requirements

Transaction data is the primary analytical domain.

The primary transaction dataset shall contain one record per transaction.

Transaction data shall support:

- Fraud-rate calculation.
- Fraud-loss analysis.
- Transaction trend analysis.
- Transaction amount analysis.
- Transaction-type analysis.
- Channel analysis.
- Merchant analysis.
- Geographic analysis.
- Device analysis.
- Transaction velocity analysis.
- Time-of-day analysis.
- Customer behavioural analysis.
- Account-level activity analysis.

Required information includes:

- transaction_id
- customer_id
- account_id
- transaction_timestamp
- transaction_amount
- currency
- transaction_type
- transaction_channel
- merchant_id
- device_id
- transaction_country
- transaction_region
- transaction_status

Fraud outcome information shall be maintained separately where appropriate to preserve a clear distinction between the original transaction and the subsequent fraud determination.

---

## 6. Merchant Data Requirements

Merchant data shall support:

- Merchant-level fraud analysis.
- Merchant-category analysis.
- Identification of merchant categories associated with higher fraud rates.

Required information includes:

- merchant_id
- merchant_name
- merchant_category
- merchant_region
- merchant_country
- merchant_status

Merchant names will be synthetic.

---

## 7. Device Data Requirements

Device data shall support:

- Device-level analysis.
- Previously unseen device identification.
- Customer-device relationship analysis.
- Device-related behavioural indicators.

Required information includes:

- device_id
- device_type
- operating_system
- device_first_seen_date
- device_region

The data shall support identification of transactions performed from devices not previously associated with a customer.

---

## 8. Geographic Data Requirements

Geographic data shall support:

- Transaction geography analysis.
- Customer-region comparison.
- Unusual geographic activity analysis.
- Country and region segmentation.

Required information includes:

- country
- region
- city_or_geographic_area
- latitude_band
- longitude_band

Exact customer addresses will not be generated.

---

## 9. Fraud Outcome Requirements

Fraud outcome data shall support:

- Confirmed fraud identification.
- Fraud-loss measurement.
- Fraud trend analysis.
- Missed-fraud analysis.
- Control effectiveness analysis.

Required information includes:

- transaction_id
- fraud_flag
- fraud_type
- fraud_confirmed_date
- fraud_loss_amount
- fraud_outcome_source

Fraud outcome records shall reference the relevant transaction using transaction_id.

Fraud outcome data represents the determination made about a transaction after the transaction occurred.

---

## 10. Fraud Control Requirements

Fraud control data shall support evaluation of existing fraud-monitoring controls.

Required information includes:

- rule_id
- rule_name
- rule_category
- rule_description
- rule_status
- alert_threshold

Each fraud rule shall have a unique identifier.

The project shall support analysis of control performance at rule level.

---

## 11. Fraud Alert Requirements

Fraud alert data shall support:

- Alert-volume analysis.
- Control detection rate.
- False-positive analysis.
- Alert conversion rate.
- Missed-fraud analysis.
- Rule-level effectiveness analysis.

Required information includes:

- alert_id
- transaction_id
- rule_id
- alert_timestamp
- alert_status
- alert_outcome
- confirmed_fraud_flag

Each alert shall reference the transaction and the fraud-monitoring rule that generated the alert.

---

## 12. Investigation Case Requirements

Investigation case data shall support:

- Investigation prioritisation.
- Open-case analysis.
- Investigation-time analysis.
- Case outcome analysis.
- Investigator workload analysis.

Required information includes:

- case_id
- alert_id
- transaction_id
- case_created_timestamp
- case_closed_timestamp
- case_priority
- case_status
- case_outcome

Cases shall reference the relevant alert and transaction where applicable.

---

## 13. Analytical Time Coverage

The synthetic analytical environment shall provide a minimum of 24 months of historical transaction activity.

The historical period shall support:

- Fraud trend analysis.
- Monthly and quarterly comparisons.
- Day-of-week analysis.
- Time-of-day analysis.
- Seasonal analysis.
- Customer behavioural baselines.
- Transaction velocity analysis.
- Historical control-performance analysis.

The exact start and end dates will be defined in the Synthetic Data Specification.

## 15. Synthetic Data Principle

All project data will be synthetic.

The generated data shall represent realistic analytical patterns, distributions and relationships while remaining entirely fictional.

The synthetic environment shall not represent real customers, real accounts, real transactions or confidential banking systems.

No real customer PII shall be used.

Synthetic data shall be designed to support meaningful fraud patterns, behavioural anomalies, control alerts and investigation outcomes rather than simply generating random records.

---

## 16. Data Design Considerations

The final data architecture shall maintain clear separation between:

- Business entities.
- Transaction events.
- Fraud outcomes.
- Fraud-monitoring controls.
- Fraud alerts.
- Investigation cases.

This separation shall support analytical reproducibility, data-quality validation, control testing and downstream SQL, Python, machine-learning and reporting activities.
