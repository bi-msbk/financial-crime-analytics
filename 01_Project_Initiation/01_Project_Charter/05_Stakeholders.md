# Project Stakeholders

The project involves business, operational, compliance, risk, analytical and technical stakeholders.

| ID      | Stakeholder | Role | Influence | Interest | Primary Need |
|---------|---|---|---|---|---|
| STK-001 | Head of Fraud | Business Sponsor | Very High | Very High | Fraud exposure, losses, trends, key drivers and recommendations |
| STK-002 | Fraud Operations Manager | Operational Owner | High | Very High | Investigation prioritisation, suspicious activity indicators and operational metrics |
| STK-003 | Financial Crime Compliance | Compliance / Control | High | High | Control effectiveness, auditability and documented methodology |
| STK-004 | Risk Manager | Risk Oversight | High | High | Emerging fraud risks, trends and control gaps |
| STK-005 | Senior Management | Executive Sponsor | Very High | High | Executive KPIs, financial impact and strategic recommendations |
| STK-006 | Data Engineer | Technical | Medium | Medium | Data requirements, data quality and pipeline requirements |
| STK-007 | Data Architect | Technical | Medium-High | Medium | Data model, entities, relationships and lineage |
| STK-008 | Data Analyst | Analytical | Medium | Very High | Business requirements, SQL analysis, analytical outputs and reporting |
| STK-009 | Data Scientist | Analytical | Medium | High | Validated fraud labels, analytical features and modelling requirements |
| STK-010 | BI Developer | Reporting | Medium | High | Approved KPIs, dimensions and dashboard requirements |

## Stakeholder Engagement Approach

### Manage Closely

- Head of Fraud
- Fraud Operations Manager
- Financial Crime Compliance
- Risk Manager

### Keep Satisfied

- Senior Management

### Keep Informed

- Data Engineer
- Data Architect
- Data Scientist
- BI Developer

### Core Delivery Stakeholder

- Data Analyst

The Data Analyst coordinates the analytical requirements, analysis, documentation and delivery across the stakeholder groups.
