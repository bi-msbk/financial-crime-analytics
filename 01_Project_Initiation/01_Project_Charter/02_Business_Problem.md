# Business Problem

## Background

Northbridge Bank has observed an increase in suspected fraudulent transaction activity across its retail banking customer base.

The Financial Crime team currently relies on transaction-monitoring controls and manual investigation processes to identify suspicious activity.

## Problem Statement

Northbridge Bank requires an analytical solution to understand fraud patterns, identify high-risk transactions and customers, evaluate the effectiveness of existing fraud controls, and provide actionable insights that can help reduce financial losses while managing false-positive volumes.

## Business Impact

If fraudulent activity is not identified effectively, the bank may experience:

- Increased financial losses
- Increased customer impact
- Higher investigation workload
- Ineffective use of fraud-monitoring controls
- Increased false-positive volumes
- Difficulty identifying emerging fraud patterns
- Reduced visibility for management and risk teams

## Business Need

The bank requires a data-driven approach that combines transaction analysis, behavioural analytics, fraud indicators, control testing, risk prioritisation and management reporting.
