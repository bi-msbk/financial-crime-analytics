We need to turn the vague business problem into measurable analytical questions.

Fraud volume

AQ-001 --> What proportion of transactions are fraudulent?

AQ-002 --> What is the total financial loss associated with confirmed fraud?

AQ-003 --> How has fraud changed over time?

Customer behaviour

AQ-004 --> Which customer segments experience the highest fraud rates?

AQ-005 --> Are fraudulent transactions associated with changes in normal customer behaviour?

AQ-006 --> Which customers demonstrate unusually high transaction activity?

Transaction behaviour

AQ-007 --> Are fraudulent transactions associated with unusually high transaction amounts?

AQ-008 --> Are particular transaction types associated with increased fraud risk?

AQ-009 --> Which channels demonstrate the highest fraud rate?

Velocity

AQ-010 --> Does a high number of transactions within a short period indicate increased fraud risk?

Geography

AQ-011  --> Are unusual geographic transaction patterns associated with fraud?

Device

AQ-012  --> Are transactions from previously unseen devices associated with fraud?

Merchant

AQ-013 --> Are certain merchant categories disproportionately affected by fraud?

Controls

AQ-014 --> How effectively do existing fraud rules detect confirmed fraud?

AQ-015 --> Which controls generate the highest number of false positives?

AQ-016 --> What proportion of confirmed fraud cases are not detected by existing controls?

Prioritisation

AQ-017 --> Can analytical risk indicators be used to prioritise cases for investigation?

Time / seasonality

AQ-018 --> Are particular days of the week, times of day, or seasonal periods associated with increased fraud activity?
