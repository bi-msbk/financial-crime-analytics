# Stakeholder Influence / Interest Map

The stakeholder map is used to determine the appropriate level of stakeholder engagement throughout the project.

| | **High Interest** | **Low Interest** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Influence** | **Manage Closely**  <br><br> Head of Fraud  <br> Fraud Operations Manager  <br> Financial Crime Compliance  <br> Risk Manager  <br> Senior Management | **Keep Satisfied**  <br><br> No major stakeholders currently identified |
| **Low Influence** | **Keep Informed**  <br><br> Data Analyst  <br> Data Scientist  <br> BI Developer  <br> Data Engineer  <br> Data Architect | **Monitor**  <br><br> No major stakeholders currently identified |

## Engagement Strategy

### Manage Closely

Maintain regular communication and involve these stakeholders in major decisions, requirements validation and approval.

### Keep Satisfied

Provide concise executive-level reporting and escalate material issues or decisions when required.

### Keep Informed

Provide relevant requirements, analytical findings, technical information and project updates.

### Monitor

No significant stakeholders currently fall into this category. This may change if the project scope expands.
