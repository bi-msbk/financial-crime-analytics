# Stakeholder Requirements

| ID | Stakeholder | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| STK-001 | Head of Fraud | Fraud exposure, trends, losses, key drivers and recommendations |
| STK-002 | Fraud Operations Manager | Case prioritisation, suspicious activity indicators and investigation metrics |
| STK-003 | Financial Crime Compliance | Control effectiveness, auditability and documented methodology |
| STK-004 | Risk Manager | Fraud trends, emerging risks and control gaps |
| STK-005 | Senior Management | Executive KPIs, financial impact and strategic recommendations |
| STK-006 | Data Engineer | Defined data requirements, quality expectations and source specifications |
| STK-007 | Data Architect | Data model, entities, relationships and lineage requirements |
| STK-008 | Data Analyst | Reliable data, analytical requirements and business context |
| STK-009 | Data Scientist | Validated fraud labels, analytical features and modelling requirements |
| STK-010 | BI Developer | Approved KPI definitions, dimensions and reporting requirements |


Questions for Head of Fraud: 
--------------------------------------
What types of fraud are currently causing the greatest financial loss?
Has fraud increased recently?
Which products/channels are most affected?
What are the current fraud KPIs?
What decisions should this analysis support?
What would success look like?

Questions for Fraud Operations:
----------------------------------------
How are suspicious transactions currently identified?
Which rules generate alerts?
How are alerts prioritised?
How many alerts are generated?
What proportion are genuine fraud?
What information does an investigator need?
What causes an investigator to escalate a case?
Which alerts are considered low-value/high-volume?
What information is currently missing?

Questions for Compliance:
--------------------------------------------
Which financial-crime controls are relevant?
What evidence must be retained?
What auditability is required?
How are control weaknesses identified?
Are there regulatory reporting requirements?
How should model/rule decisions be explained?

Questions for Data Engineering:
-------------------------------------------------
Where does transaction data originate?
How frequently is it refreshed?
What is the transaction volume?
What customer/account data is available?
Are device identifiers available?
Are merchant identifiers available?
Are geographic fields available?
What data-quality problems exist?
Are historical records available?
How is data accessed?

Risk Manager
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Which fraud risks are currently considered highest priority?
What risk thresholds are currently used?
How are emerging risks identified?
What reporting does Risk currently receive?
What constitutes a material fraud/control issue?

Analytics Lead
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What analytical standards should be followed?
What level of reproducibility is required?
What tools/platforms are approved?
How should analytical outputs be validated?
What documentation is required before delivery?
